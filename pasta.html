How To Make Pasta

Step 1

  • Get your pasta

Step 2

  • Boil a pot of water

Step 3

  • After 5-10 minutes, add pasta to the water

Step 4

  • Add butter to the pasta and stir it around

Step 5

  • Once soft enough to bite, dump it in a strainer and pour out the water into the sink

Step 6

  • Put the pasta into a bowl and eat!
