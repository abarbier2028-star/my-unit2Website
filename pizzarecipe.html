How to Make Pizza

Step 1

  • Get your dough and roll it out

Step 2

  • Get tomato sauce and spread it on top of the dough

Step 3

  • Sprinkle cheese on top of the sauce

Step 4

  • Add any toppings you want

Step 5

  • Put it in the oven

Step 6

  • Take it out of the oven after 15 minutes and eat!
